# RewardAI Professional Product Blueprint

## Product positioning

RewardAI Professional turns Excel-based salary review processes into consultant-grade compensation recommendations, budget scenarios and leadership-ready reports.

## Target users

- Rewards Consultant
- CHRO / HR Head
- Total Rewards Head
- CFO / Finance Manager
- HR Business Partner
- NRC / Board committee support team

## Professional modules

1. Executive Dashboard
2. Upload & Validation Center
3. Pay Positioning
4. Market Benchmark Analyzer
5. Merit Planner
6. Promotion Planner
7. Lump-Sum Planner
8. Scenario Simulator
9. Budget & Governance
10. AI Reports
11. Export Center

## Key formulas

- Compa-Ratio = Current Base Salary / Salary Range Midpoint
- Range Penetration = (Current Salary - Minimum) / (Maximum - Minimum)
- Merit Amount = Current Base Salary × Merit Percent
- Promotion Increase = MAX(Current Salary × Promotion %, New Grade Minimum - Current Salary)
- Market Ratio P50 = Current Salary / Market P50
- Budget Utilization = Total Proposed Reward Cost / Approved Total Budget

## Professional USP

RewardAI Professional helps HR teams and rewards consultants deliver complete salary review cycles with pay positioning, market benchmarking, merit planning, promotion analysis, budget scenarios, risk governance and executive-ready reports.
