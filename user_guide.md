# RewardAI Professional User Guide

## Step 1: Run the app

Run `python -m streamlit run app.py` or double-click `run_rewardai_professional.bat` on Windows.

## Step 2: Load sample data

Use the sidebar button `Load Sample Data`, or download the sample workbook and upload it.

## Step 3: Prepare your own data

Download the blank template and fill these sheets:

- Employee_Data
- Salary_Structure
- Merit_Matrix
- Budget_Assumptions
- Market_Benchmark
- Promotion_Nominations
- Critical_Roles

Do not change sheet names or column names in the first version.

## Step 4: Validate

Open Upload & Validation to check missing columns, duplicate IDs, grade mapping, rating mapping and other data issues.

## Step 5: Review modules

- Executive Dashboard: overall summary
- Pay Positioning: compa-ratio and range penetration
- Market Benchmark: internal salary vs market P50/P75
- Merit Planner: merit % and new salary calculation
- Promotion Planner: promotion cost and new-grade fit
- Lump-Sum Planner: employees near or above maximum
- Scenario Simulator: compare compensation strategies
- Budget & Governance: budget utilization and risk flags

## Step 6: Generate reports

Use AI Reports to create CHRO, CFO, NRC, HRBP, consultant and manager communication outputs.

## Step 7: Export

Use Export Center to download the full Excel analysis and CSV outputs.
