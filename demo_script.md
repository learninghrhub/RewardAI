# RewardAI Professional Demo Script

## Opening

Many companies still run salary reviews manually in Excel. The challenge is not Excel itself; the challenge is that salary decisions often lack structured pay positioning, budget governance, market comparison and leadership-ready explanation.

RewardAI Professional solves this by turning salary data into compensation recommendations, risk flags, budget scenarios and CHRO/CFO/NRC-ready reports.

## Demo flow

1. Start on the Executive Dashboard.
2. Show employee count, budget utilization and risk flags.
3. Move to Pay Positioning and explain compa-ratio and range penetration.
4. Move to Market Benchmark and show market P50/P75 gaps.
5. Move to Merit Planner and show how performance + compa-zone drives merit %.
6. Move to Promotion Planner and show new grade movement.
7. Move to Lump-Sum Planner and explain employees at or above maximum.
8. Move to Scenario Simulator and compare Base, Conservative, High Differentiation and Critical Talent Focus.
9. Open AI Reports and generate NRC Approval Paper.
10. Export the full analysis.

## Closing message

RewardAI Professional helps HR and rewards consultants move from manual salary review to structured, fair, affordable and governance-ready pay decisions.
